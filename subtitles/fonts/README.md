# Fonts

Open-licence fonts only, used by the caption renderer: Noto Sans (and its Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Devanagari, Bengali, Gujarati, Gurmukhi families),
Anek (Indic bold weights), Montserrat, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Permanent Marker, Baloo Tamma 2, Catamaran and Ramabhadra. They come from Google Fonts and are
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Permanent Marker: Apache 2.0). Do not add a font here unless its licence allows redistribution; the commercial
`HelveticaNeue-Bold`, `CooperStd-Black` and `The Bold Font` that sat next to them in the source project are deliberately left out.
